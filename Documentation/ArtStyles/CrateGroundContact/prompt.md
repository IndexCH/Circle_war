# 箱底盐壳过渡素材

生成方式：内置 image_gen，新生成独立透明箱底过渡素材。共用该素材适配三种像素箱子，保留箱体原图和还原色表。

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a single transparent pixel-art GROUND CONTACT OVERLAY sprite for the bases of supply crates in a 2D Unity game.
Create only one small shallow patch of pale saline soil, salt crust and tiny stones, designed to sit under a crate and blend its hard bottom edge into an existing salt-crusted ground. No crate or other prop in the output.

Shape and viewpoint: a very low irregular elongated ground seam seen nearly side-on, width about SIX TIMES its height. The center has a narrow broken band of pale off-white salt crust, tiny angular gray-beige pebbles and a little compact gray-brown dirt; a few small chips rise above the line to overlap the lowest edge of a box. A restrained narrow dark crevice along the upper-center implies contact shadow where the crate would rest. The lower edge tapers into scattered sparse pixels so it can overlap existing terrain naturally. The two ends taper irregularly. Vary the chips gently, keep the center mostly flat. It is a thin patch, NOT a raised pedestal, mound, boulder, platform, island or thick cross-section of terrain. No large rocks.

Palette must match pale salt-crusted stone ground: salt highlights warm off-white #DEDACD, midtone gray-beige #B5B0A2, rock shadow warm gray #77736A, small crevice shadow gray-brown #514D44. Neutral pale crust dominates, muted soil is secondary. No pink, purple, green grass, saturated yellow, orange glow, moss or vegetation. Soft neutral daylight; no cast shadow extending outside the patch.

Pixel-art rendering: small clean square pixel clusters, hard stepped edges, limited-color detailed but restrained stone shapes, suitable for a Terraria-like game. Fine grain for a patch ultimately displayed about 100–200 screen pixels wide. No soft blur, gradients, painterly strokes or photographic grain. Do not use a thick dark outline.
Composition: one centered standalone strip, fully visible, comfortable TRANSPARENT margin on every side, horizontal wide canvas. TRUE RGBA transparent background with alpha zero outside the patch; no white or black canvas, no checkerboard pattern painted into pixels. No text, labels, border, UI, characters, crate, metal pieces, or other assets. Deliver exactly one ground-contact patch.
