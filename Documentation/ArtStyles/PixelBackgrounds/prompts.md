## pixel-background-eye-level-v1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: an original full-bleed 2D pixel-art game background texture for a Unity side-view game, inspired by the clean layered environmental backgrounds of Terraria, not a screenshot or a copy of an existing game asset. Single image only, wide 16:9 landscape composition, ideally 1920x1080. Authentic carefully placed square pixel clusters, fine consistent pixel grid, clean stepped silhouettes, restrained 16-bit color palette, large calm color shapes. Keep the detail scale consistent across the image, visually similar to a 960x540 pixel-art canvas enlarged with nearest-neighbor. Bright but restrained cool daylight: clear blue, soft cyan, muted teal and white with pale blue/lavender shadows. No yellow or orange color cast. No painterly brush strokes, no smooth airbrushing, no blurry edges, no speckled dithering or noisy microtexture. Opaque background, no transparency. No UI, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no border, no player, no monsters, no foreground props, no circular ground.
Primary request: a level, eye-height view across a tranquil distant landscape. A straight level horizon, open blue sky occupying roughly the upper two thirds, several modest puffy white cloud clusters, a few gently layered distant blue and blue-green hills and low mountain silhouettes below the horizon, with a simple faraway treeline along the lower edge. Horizontal side-scroller parallax-background design: distant land layers read as calm bands and silhouettes rather than a detailed foreground. The viewer looks straight ahead, not downward and not upward; no overhead view, no dramatic perspective, no close-up ground, no water reflections. The lower edge remains a complete quiet landscape layer, with open space through the central sky so gameplay sprites remain legible. Cloud shapes have only two or three shades and the hills have simple limited-color contour planes. Maintain a friendly expansive Terraria-like pixel world mood.

Single image only, wide 16:9 landscape composition, ideally 1920x1080. Authentic carefully placed square pixel clusters, fine consistent pixel grid, clean stepped silhouettes, restrained 16-bit color palette, large calm color shapes. Keep the detail scale consistent across the image, visually similar to a 960x540 pixel-art canvas enlarged with nearest-neighbor. Primary request: looking upward into a wide open daytime sky, with soft large white cumulus cloud banks seen from below, distributed naturally around generous areas of clean blue sky. SKY AND CLOUDS ONLY: absolutely no horizon, mountains, ground, treeline, landscape, buildings, celestial bodies, birds or objects. The upward camera angle should be unmistakable, as if the player tilted their view up from the level landscape background. Keep a calm open central patch of blue sky and clouds flowing organically across the edges, without a radial tunnel or fisheye distortion.
